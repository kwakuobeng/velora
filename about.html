Built for DFW homeowners who want clarity

Velora is a lean residential landscaping startup: affordable rates, satellite-assisted quoting, and reliable scheduling across Dallas–Fort Worth.

Our approach

Most landscaping quotes still start with a long wait for someone to drive out, walk the yard, and “get back to you.” Velora flips that. You request a quote online, we measure your property from satellite imagery, and you get a flat-rate price you can trust before we schedule a visit.

We’re focused on four things homeowners actually need: mowing, yard cleanups, mulch and flower beds, and hedge trimming. No bloated menu of services we can’t staff well — just reliable work at a clear price.

Whether you’re in Plano, Fort Worth, or somewhere in between, the process is the same: request → measure → flat rate → scheduled service.
