Services

Yard care without the vague estimate.

Four focused residential services, priced from the size and scope of your property.

Lawn Mowing

Keep your grass at a healthy height with consistent, scheduled mows. Every visit includes perimeter edging and a blow-off of walkways and driveways so the finish looks intentional — not rushed.

Choose weekly or bi-weekly based on season and growth. We price by measured lawn size.

  • Weekly or bi-weekly
  • Edging included
  • Walkway blow-off
  • Flat-rate by lot size

Yard Cleanups

When leaves pile up, storms leave debris, or a yard has been neglected, a one-time cleanup resets the property. We clear leaves, sticks, and loose debris, then haul it away.

Ideal before listing a home, after seasonal weather, or as a spring/fall reset.

  • Leaf & debris removal
  • Haul-away included
  • Seasonal resets
  • One-time or add-on

Mulch & Flower Bed Installs

Fresh mulch holds moisture, suppresses weeds, and makes beds look finished. We install mulch over existing beds or help build simple flower bed layouts with clean edges.

Tell us the look you’re after and share photos — we’ll quote materials and labor as a flat package.

  • Mulch delivery & install
  • Bed edging
  • Simple plant installs
  • Package pricing

Hedge Trimming

We shape hedges to clean lines, remove dead growth, and tidy the base so mulch and beds show through.

Available as a one-time shape-up or paired with your mowing schedule.

  • Shape & maintain
  • Dead growth removal
  • Cleanup after trim
  • One-time or recurring
